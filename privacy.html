Nebula Patrol Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Voltrion Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") handles information when you play Nebula Patrol (package / bundle ID: com.voltrion.nebulapatrol) on Android and iOS.

Summary

  • No sign-up is required. The game does not ask for your name or email.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and measures how the game is played (Google Analytics for Firebase).
  • On Android, the game may sign you in automatically with Google Play Games to enable leaderboards, achievements and saved progress.
  • Your high score and your sound and vibration settings are stored on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you for personal information. The following is collected automatically by the Google services built into the game:

  • Device identifiers: advertising ID (Android Advertising ID; on iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking), Firebase app instance ID.
  • Device and app information: device model, OS version, language, app version.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Gameplay events: for example run start and end, wave reached, score, run length, use of the continue option, and whether an ad was shown or completed.
  • Ad interactions: ad impressions, clicks and the related revenue reported by the ad network.
  • Google Play Games (Android): if you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID, gamer profile name and avatar, scores you submit to leaderboards, achievements you unlock, and, if you use saved progress, your game save data stored in the app's private Google Drive folder. We do not receive your email address or access other files in your Google Drive.

2. How we use it

  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch for a continue.
  • To provide leaderboards, achievements and saved progress through Google Play Games.
  • To understand how players use the game (for example how long runs last and where players stop) so we can fix problems and improve difficulty and fun.
  • To measure ad performance and keep the game free.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of advertising.

3. Third-party services

The game uses the following services, which process data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Games Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ad networks may show personalized ads based on your advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

App Tracking Transparency (iOS): on iOS, the game asks for your permission before accessing the IDFA. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not accessed and you still see ads, but they are not personalized using it. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Consent (EEA, UK and other regions where required): before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent message so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY OPTIONS button on the title screen.

Google Play Games: you can sign out of Google Play Games, change your profile visibility, or delete your Play Games data at any time in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above for advertising, analytics and game services, or when required by law. Leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile name may be visible to other players according to your Google Play Games visibility settings.

5. Retention

Analytics event data is kept for a limited period (up to 14 months) and then deleted or aggregated. Data stored only on your device (high score, settings) is removed when you uninstall the game. Play Games data is kept by Google until you delete it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the game stops all further collection.
  • You can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • You can ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, or ask any question about your data, by emailing cs@voltrion.io with the subject "Nebula Patrol data request". We reply within 30 days.
  • If you live in a region with specific privacy laws (such as the EU/UK GDPR, or California CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your data and to object to certain processing. Contact us at the address above to use them.

7. Children

Nebula Patrol is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the service providers listed above.

9. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country. Google applies appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
